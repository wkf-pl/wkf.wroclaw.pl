# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary public audiences are:

- Prospective members who are interested in the club's activities and need enough confidence, practical information, and motivation to join.
- People evaluating whether Wrocławski Klub Fantastyki is a trustworthy organization, especially public officials assessing grant applications and staff of cultural institutions considering cooperation or venue rental.
- Fantasy enthusiasts, both members of other clubs and unaffiliated people, among whom WKF needs to build recognition and a strong reputation.

Club members, editors, and administrators are secondary operational users. They maintain content and use the website as a communication and information-distribution platform for the community.

## Product Purpose

WKF Online is the official website and publishing platform of Wrocławski Klub Fantastyki. It exists to integrate fantasy fans, promote fantasy-related creative work, communicate the club's activities, and help people and institutions understand how to engage with the organization.

Product success means:

- Establishing and sustaining a strong, credible WKF reputation that supports relationships with cultural institutions, grant funders, service providers, and other partners.
- Attracting and retaining a strong community of fantasy fans.
- Providing that community with a dependable platform for communication and information distribution.

## Positioning

WKF is a young organization with legal personality. In addition to running its own activities, it can act as an organizational hub for smaller fantasy initiatives that do not have legal personality of their own. The website must make that institutional credibility and community-enabling role understandable without pretending that the young club already has an extensive track record.

## Operating Context

Prospective members use the public website to learn what the club does, see current activities and events, and decide whether to join. Institutional visitors use it as part of due diligence before grants, cooperation, or venue arrangements. The broader fantasy community uses it to follow news, events, club sections, people, and initiatives.

Authorized club users publish and maintain this information through the integrated Payload administration panel. Public content is presented in Polish and includes pages, news, events and calendar feeds, club sections, member profiles, partners, documents, and media.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The product combines a public Next.js website with a Payload content-management and administration interface.
- The current information architecture is an initial foundation and is expected to expand substantially as the organization develops.
- The organization is young and currently has limited achievements and proof to present. Future work must not compensate by inventing accomplishments, partnerships, testimonials, impact figures, or institutional recognition.
- Public communication must support both community participation and institutional trust.
- Editorial roles, permissions, drafts, and published states support controlled content maintenance.
- The public-facing language is Polish.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the official name: Wrocławski Klub Fantastyki (WKF).
- Preserve the approved logo assets at `public/assets/logo-color.webp` and `public/assets/logo-black.webp` unless the organization explicitly changes its identity.
- Communicate as a community and cultural organization rather than as a commercial entertainment brand.
- Be credible, factual, welcoming to new people, and grounded in the club's real activity.
- Treat the club's legal personality and support for smaller initiatives as important, factual parts of its identity.

## Evidence on Hand

- Approved WKF logo assets exist at `public/assets/logo-color.webp` and `public/assets/logo-black.webp`.
- The repository contains implemented content structures for pages, posts, events, club sections, member profiles, partners, documents, and media.
- Content created by `scripts/seed.ts` is demonstrative and must not be treated as evidence of real activities, achievements, or institutional relationships.
- The present website structure is a genuine starting point for organizing information, but its breadth does not demonstrate a mature organizational track record.
- No verified testimonials, case studies, impact metrics, grant successes, press coverage, or partner endorsements are established in the repository. Future work must use only evidence explicitly supplied or confirmed by WKF.

## Product Principles

1. Earn trust through verifiable information and institutional clarity.
2. Make it easy for interested people to understand the club and move toward participation.
3. Strengthen the fantasy community by making activities and information easy to discover and share.
4. Represent the organization's current maturity honestly while leaving room for its information architecture to grow.
5. Use WKF's legal capacity to make smaller community initiatives more viable and visible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Accessibility is a binding product requirement. The website must remain usable by people with diverse access needs and follow WCAG; the exact WCAG version and conformance level remain to be confirmed. Public communication should be understandable to newcomers and should not assume prior membership or familiarity with club structures.
